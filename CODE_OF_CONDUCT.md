# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment.

Be considerate, give actionable technical feedback, respect differing
experience levels, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and sustained
disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository maintainers.
